About Focus Writer

Welcome to Focus Writer

Focus Writer is a distraction-free writing environment designed to help you achieve your writing goals. Whether you're working on a novel, blog post, or daily journal, Focus Writer keeps you focused and productive.

Key Features

  • Timer Mode: Set a time goal for your writing session
  • Word Count Mode: Set a target word count to achieve
  • Rich Text Editing: Format your text with headers, bold, italic, and more
  • Auto-Save: Never lose your work with automatic saving
  • Session Tracking: Monitor your writing progress over time
  • Distraction-Free: Full-screen mode to eliminate distractions

How to Use

Getting Started

1. Choose your writing mode: Timer or Word Count

2. Set your goal using the slider

3. Select whether to start a new document, open an existing one, or continue your most recent work

4. Click "Start Writing Session" to begin

During Your Session

  • Your progress is displayed at the top of the editor
  • Use the formatting toolbar to style your text
  • Click "Save" to manually save (auto-save happens every 5 seconds)
  • Click "Pause" to temporarily stop the timer
  • When you reach your goal, choose to continue, freewrite, or finish

Tips for Success

  • Set realistic goals - start small and build up
  • Write consistently - daily writing builds momentum
  • Don't edit while writing - focus on getting words down
  • Use timer mode for sprints, word count mode for daily goals
  • Take breaks between sessions to stay fresh
